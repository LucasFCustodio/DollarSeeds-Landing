What's new in DollarSeeds

Release notes, newest first. For what's coming next, tell us what you want — real requests drive the roadmap.

Debt payoff goals · August 2026

  • New: debt payoff goals. Point a goal at a balance instead of a purchase and chip away at what you owe on the same screen where your savings goals grow.
  • New lessons added, including Tithing and Entrepreneurship.

v1.0 — App Store launch · August 7, 2026

DollarSeeds ships on the App Store. The launch feature set:

  • Automatic income splitting across Needs, Wants, and Savings the moment income is logged.
  • Three budgeting types — 50/30/20, Wealth Builder, and Firm Foundation — every one saving at least 20%.
  • Expense tracking with categories and subcategories.
  • Savings goals with target amounts, timelines, and a weekly set-aside pace.
  • Budget Health score on the dashboard.
  • Scripture-rooted lessons, including Saving for the Unexpected and Giving It All to God.
  • No bank login — by design. Here's why.
