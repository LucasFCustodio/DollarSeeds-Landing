One-line description

DollarSeeds is a free Christian budgeting app for iPhone that splits income automatically across Needs, Wants, and Savings — no bank login required — and teaches biblical stewardship through built-in lessons.

Boilerplate

DollarSeeds is a personal budgeting app for iPhone built on a simple conviction: every dollar is a seed, and where you plant it determines what grows. The app divides income automatically across Needs, Wants, and Savings using one of three budgeting types — the balanced 50/30/20, the aggressive Wealth Builder (50% savings rate), and the transitional Firm Foundation for tight seasons — all sharing a minimum 20% savings target. Alongside the budget, DollarSeeds ships Scripture-rooted lessons on tithing, entrepreneurship, saving, and giving. The app never connects to bank accounts; users log income and expenses themselves. DollarSeeds launched on the App Store on August 7, 2026, and is built by Lucas Custodio, a developer with a background in financial-services automation and web development. It is free to download at apps.apple.com.
