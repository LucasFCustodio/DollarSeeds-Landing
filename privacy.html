1. Who we are

DollarSeeds ("DollarSeeds", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by Lucas Fracasso Custodio, an independent developer. For the purposes of data protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, Lucas Fracasso Custodio is the "data controller" responsible for your personal information.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, you can reach us at lucasquality555@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the DollarSeeds mobile application on Apple iOS and Google Android, and to this website. It does not apply to third-party services we rely on, which handle data under their own privacy policies (linked in the "How information is shared" section below).

3. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to function and to stay reliable. The categories are:

a. Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and an authentication identifier. This is used to sign you in and to associate your data with your account. Authentication and account records are managed through our backend provider, Supabase.

b. Financial information you enter

DollarSeeds stores the budgeting information you choose to enter, including income entries (such as an income source label, amount, and type), expenses (such as a title, amount, and category — Needs, Wants, or Goals), and savings and debt goals and their transactions. This information is stored in our own database (Supabase) so the app can show you your budget. It is never shared with analytics providers, advertisers, or data brokers.

c. Usage and analytics data

To understand how the app is used and improve it, we collect a limited, deliberate set of behavioral events through PostHog — for example, that an expense was logged (including only its category, such as "Needs" or "Wants"), that a lesson was opened, or how far a lesson video was watched. These events are tied to a pseudonymous account identifier, not to your name or email. Dollar amounts and other financial values are never included in analytics events. Automatic capture of screen taps and session replay (screen recording) are disabled.

d. Diagnostics and crash data

To keep the app stable, we use Sentry to collect crash reports and error diagnostics, which may include device model, operating system version, app version, and technical details about the error (such as a stack trace). This helps us find and fix problems.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for third-party or cross-app advertising, and the app contains no ad SDKs.
  • We do not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not record your screen or session, and analytics autocapture is turned off.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data.
  • We do not send your financial figures (incomes, expenses, balances, goals) to any analytics or advertising service.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the app: authenticate you, store and display your budget, income, expenses, goals, and lessons progress.
  • To improve the app: understand which features are used and where users encounter friction, using pseudonymous analytics.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure: detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and errors.
  • To communicate with you: respond to support requests you send us.
  • To comply with the law: meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your financial information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not provide it to third parties for their own purposes.

6. Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the app's core functionality once you create an account (account and financial data).
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the app secure and reliable (diagnostics) and to improve it (pseudonymous analytics), balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — where required by law, for any optional processing; you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process data to comply with applicable law.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell your data. We share limited information only with the service providers ("sub-processors") that make the app work, and only for the purposes described above. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

Supabase Authentication, database & hosting Account email, authentication identifier, the financial data you enter supabase.com/privacy
PostHog Product analytics Pseudonymous account ID and behavioral events (no financial amounts, no email) posthog.com/privacy

We may also disclose information if required by law, to comply with a legal process, to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others, or in connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), in which case we will notify you of any material change to how your data is handled.

8. Data retention

We keep your account and financial data for as long as your account is active, so the app can show your history over time. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal and financial data from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Pseudonymous analytics and crash diagnostics are retained on a rolling basis according to our providers' standard retention periods.

9. How we protect your data

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) that encrypts data at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your authenticated account, and access to production systems is limited. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and to promptly address issues if they arise.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and personal data.
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection / restriction — object to or ask us to restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing" — we do not sell or share your data for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you may still contact us with any concern.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at lucasquality555@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Account & data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated personal and financial data at any time directly within the app, using the Delete Account option in the Settings screen. You may also request deletion by emailing lucasquality555@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Once processed, your account and its financial records are removed from our database, subject to the limited legal exceptions described in the Data retention section.

12. Children's privacy

DollarSeeds is not directed to children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and older (and 16 and older where required by local law, such as parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
