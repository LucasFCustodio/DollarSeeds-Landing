Support

How can we help?

Questions, bugs, ideas, or anything in between — DollarSeeds is built by one person who reads every message.

Contact

Email lucasquality555@gmail.com from any address — or from your account email if the question is about your account.

Include your iPhone model and iOS version when reporting a bug — it usually cuts the back-and-forth in half.

Common questions

Does DollarSeeds connect to my bank?

No — the app never asks for bank credentials and can't see your accounts. You log income and expenses yourself. Details on the security page.

My income split looks different than I expected — why?

The split follows your selected budgeting type: 50/30/20, Wealth Builder (30/20/50), or Firm Foundation (70/10/20). Check which one is active in the app, and see the budgeting types guide for what each split means.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account → type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent. If you can't sign in, email us instead — full steps on the delete account page.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — DollarSeeds is currently iPhone-only. If you want Android, say so by email; real requests drive the roadmap.

How do I request a feature?

Email it. Feature ideas from real users have already shaped the app — debt payoff goals among them. See what's shipped in the changelog.

Policies
