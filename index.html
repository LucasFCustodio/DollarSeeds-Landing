Who It's For

Built for people chasing financial freedom

DollarSeeds is for anyone chasing financial freedom — people getting out of debt, people multiplying what they already have, and people aiming at goals bigger than next month's bills.

It's also for people who don't want money just to fill their pockets, but to use it in a way that pleases God and blesses the people around them. If that's you, this app was built with you in mind. If it isn't, the budgeting still works — the discipline doesn't check your beliefs at the door.

What DollarSeeds replaces

  • Lack of control Money leaves the account faster than you decided it should.
  • Lack of direction No answer to "what is this money actually for?"
  • Lack of planning Every month starts from zero, and ends by accident.
  • Lack of self-knowledge No real idea where the money actually goes.

All four replaced with the same thing: financial control.

How It Works

Three steps, every month

  1. Log your income

    Paycheck, side gig, gift, refund — log it in seconds when it arrives. Every harvest counts.

  2. It splits automatically

    DollarSeeds divides each amount across your three buckets the moment you log it — no math, no spreadsheet.

    NeedsWantsSavings

  3. Track and adjust

    Every expense lands in a category with a target. See what's left, catch overspending early, and adjust before the month gets away from you.

The split isn't one-size-fits-all. DollarSeeds ships three budgeting types — pick the one that matches your season of life.

Budgeting Types

Three budgeting types. One philosophy.

Every track saves at least 20% of what you earn. What changes is how much of the rest goes to needs versus wants. Read the three profiles — you'll recognize yours in about five seconds.

Every track saves at least 20%

50/30/20

50 Needs · 30 Wants · 20 Savings

The balanced track. Half of every dollar covers the essentials — rent, groceries, bills, the things you need to live and serve faithfully where you are. Thirty percent goes to the joys: dining, travel, lifestyle, enjoying God's gifts without letting them consume you. Twenty percent gets planted for the future.

Fits you if: your income is steady and comfortably covers your needs with room to spare. Read the full 50/30/20 guide →

Wealth Builder

30 Needs · 20 Wants · 50 Savings

Half of everything you earn goes to savings. This is the track for people who earn well above what their needs cost and want that surplus working — invested into future income streams, aimed at goals most budgets never get to.

It's also the right fit for college students living with their parents: few fixed needs, and years for every invested dollar to compound.

Fits you if: your needs take a small share of your income and you want the rest building something.

Firm Foundation

70 Needs · 10 Wants · 20 Savings

For the season when needs eat most of the paycheck. Firm Foundation makes room for that honestly — 70% to needs — but it refuses to give up the 20% savings target. That 20% is held on purpose: it's the margin that builds enough cushion to grow your finances and find income that comfortably covers your costs.

This track is transitional by design. The goal is to graduate out of it — into 50/30/20, and one day maybe Wealth Builder.

Fits you if: your salary barely covers your needs right now — and you're building your way out.

Want the deeper version, with worked examples? See the full budgeting types guide or start with the 50/30/20 rule.

Why DollarSeeds

The name behind the mission

A seed is small. It requires patience, intention, and care before it ever becomes something visible. That's exactly how finances work — not through sudden windfalls, but through faithful, daily decisions that compound over time.

The name DollarSeeds comes from that idea. Every dollar you earn is a seed. Where you plant it — how you tend it — determines what grows. Spend carelessly and nothing takes root. Steward it well, and it becomes provision, security, and generosity.

The Bible speaks about money more than almost any other topic. Yet for many Christians, faith and finances remain in separate rooms. DollarSeeds was built to open the door between them — not just to budget better, but to steward faithfully. And if you don't share the faith, the discipline still holds: the app never asks you to believe anything to budget well. It reads as conviction, not a gate.

"Little by little, it grows."

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is DollarSeeds free?

Yes — DollarSeeds is free to download on the App Store.

Download it, pick a budgeting type, and start logging income the same day.

Does DollarSeeds connect to my bank account?

No — DollarSeeds never asks for your bank login, and it can't see your accounts.

You log income and expenses yourself, in a few seconds each. That's a deliberate choice: logging a purchase by hand is the moment you actually feel where your money goes, and it means your credentials are never shared with anyone. Read more on our security page.

Is DollarSeeds only for Christians?

No — anyone can use DollarSeeds; the budgeting system works the same regardless of what you believe.

The app is unapologetically rooted in biblical stewardship — you'll see Scripture on the dashboard and faith-based lessons inside. But nothing is gated behind belief, and the Needs / Wants / Savings discipline stands on its own.

What are the three budgeting types in DollarSeeds?

DollarSeeds ships three budgeting types: 50/30/20 (balanced), Wealth Builder (30/20/50, for aggressive savers), and Firm Foundation (70/10/20, for tight seasons).

Every one of them saves at least 20% of your income — what changes is the balance between needs and wants. See the comparison above or the full budgeting types guide.

What is the 50/30/20 budget rule?

The 50/30/20 rule splits your after-tax income into 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings.

Needs are the essentials — rent, groceries, bills. Wants are the joys — dining out, travel, hobbies. Savings is the future — emergency fund, goals, investing, generosity. DollarSeeds applies the split automatically every time you log income. Full worked example on our 50/30/20 rule page.

Can I track debt payoff with DollarSeeds?

Yes — DollarSeeds lets you create debt payoff goals and chip away at a balance the same way you'd fund a savings goal.

Point a goal at what you owe, set the pace, and watch the balance shrink from the same Savings screen where your other goals grow.

Is my financial data private?

Yes — your financial data belongs to you, is never sold, and the app holds no bank credentials that could be exposed.

Only what you type in is stored, and you can permanently delete your account and every record with it at any time. The plain-language version is on our security page; the legal version is the privacy policy.

Is there an Android version of DollarSeeds?

Not yet — DollarSeeds is currently iPhone-only.

An Android version depends on how the iOS app grows. If you want it, tell us — real requests are how it gets prioritized.

How do I delete my DollarSeeds account?

Open the app, go to Settings, tap Delete Account, and type DELETE to confirm — deletion is immediate and permanent.

Every income, expense, savings, and goal record tied to the account is removed. If you can't sign in, you can request deletion by email instead — full steps on the delete account page.
